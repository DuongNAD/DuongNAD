## About

Software Engineering student building **AI/ML-powered products**, **living systems simulations**, and data-driven systems with **Python**, **TypeScript**, **SQL**, and **Rust**. Team Lead of **MindSync** at the **Vietnam AI Innovation Challenge 2026**, owning architecture and delivery of a deployable public-service AI assistant.

I ship end-to-end work across **agentic AI**, **artificial life & evolutionary computation**, **local LLM inference**, **computer vision**, and **statistical experimentation** — with a hard bias toward reproducibility, model evaluation, and measurable outcomes.

- Education: **FPT University** (Software Engineering) & **APTECH** (ADSE Diploma)
- Passions: Artificial Life & Ecosystem simulations, Agentic systems, Local LLMs, and Botanical / Natural Modeling
- Ask me about: MCP agents, RAG / vector search, deterministic simulation, and shipping under a 48-hour deadline
- Based in **Hanoi, Vietnam**
